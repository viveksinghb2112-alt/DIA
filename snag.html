The app rebuilds a walkable 3D photo structure (and a 360 view if you include equirectangular panoramas). Then click every crack, switch, or tile flaw, write what must be rectified, and generate the report.

A true survey-grade mesh still needs a GPU tool (Polycam etc.). This app builds the inspection walkthrough in the browser from what you upload, and logs snags on that structure.
